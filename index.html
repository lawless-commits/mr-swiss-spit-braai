FAQ

Questions hosts ask us

How early does the team arrive to start the coals and roast?

A spit braai is cooked slowly, so we usually arrive several hours before serving time — typically [e.g. 4–5 hours] depending on the meat and guest count. We’ll confirm the exact arrival time with you when we finalise your booking.

Do you offer delivery/drop-off as well as full on-site spit and carving service?

Yes, both. Choose drop-off if you’d like the cooked meat and sides delivered ready to serve, or full on-site service where we roast at your venue and carve and serve for your guests.

How far in advance should we book for weekends or peak seasons?

Weekends, December and public holidays fill up fast — we recommend booking [e.g. 3–4 weeks] ahead. For other dates, send us a message anyway; we’re flexible and will always try to help at shorter notice.

Can you accommodate dietary preferences such as halaal-friendly sourcing or vegetarian sides?

Please tell us about any dietary needs when you request a quote. We can include vegetarian sides and discuss halaal-friendly meat sourcing and preparation for your event.
